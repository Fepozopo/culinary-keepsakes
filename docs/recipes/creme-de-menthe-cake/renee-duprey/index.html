Creme de Menthe Cake

Renee DuPrey

Description

This easy, showstopper dessert combines a light white cake with a bright mint kick — creme de menthe is folded into the batter, then the cooled cake is layered with rich chocolate fudge and a minty whipped topping. The result is a creamy, mint-chocolate treat that’s refreshing yet indulgent, perfect for holidays, dinner parties, or St. Patrick’s Day celebrations.

Tips: Use creme de menthe liqueur for authentic flavor, or substitute 1–2 teaspoons peppermint extract mixed with a little cream or milk for a non‑alcoholic version (adjust to taste). Chill the cake so the layers set cleanly, and garnish with chocolate shavings, crushed peppermint candies, or a few fresh mint leaves just before serving. Store refrigerated and enjoy cold.

Ingredients

  • 1 box White cake mix
  • 1/3 c Creme de Menthe
  • Topping:
  • 1 jar Hershey’s fudge topping
  • 1 tub Cool Whip
  • 1/3 c Creme de Menthe

Instructions

  1. Bake: Prepare cake mix as directed on the box. Add 1/3 c Creme de Menthe to the batter. Bake as directed and cool.
  2. Layer 1: Spread the jar of fudge topping over the cooled cake.
  3. Layer 2: Mix the Cool Whip and the remaining 1/3 c Creme de Menthe together. Spread this over the fudge layer.
  4. Storage: Keep in the refrigerator.
