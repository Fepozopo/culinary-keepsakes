Bear Necessities

Sassenach Spirits

Description

Crisp, bold, and not to be underestimated. Mix one in, settle in, and get ready for what’s coming.

Ingredients

  • 2 ounces Sassenach Gin
  • 1 ounce lime juice
  • 1/2 ounce simple syrup
  • 6–8 mint leaves
  • Soda, for topping
