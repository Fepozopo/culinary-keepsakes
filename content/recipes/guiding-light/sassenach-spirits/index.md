# Guiding Light

#### [Sassenach Spirits](/authors/sassenach-spirits/)

![Guiding Light](/images/guiding-light.webp)

### Description

A drink that transcends time…just like the story.

### Ingredients

- 2 ounces Sassenach Scottish Vodka
- 1.5 ounces pistachio coconut purée
- 0.25 ounce cacao
- 0.25 ounce vanilla simple syrup
- Fresh mint
