### Description

A comforting twist on classic meatloaf that brings pizza flavors to the dinner table. Tomato soup, oregano, and garlic create a savory, saucy base while bread cubes keep the loaf tender and moist. Topped with melted mozzarella for a gooey finish.

### Ingredients

- 1 (10 1/2 oz) can Tomato soup
- 1/4 c Water
- 1/2 tsp Oregano, crushed
- 1 clove Garlic, minced
- 1 c Small bread cubes
- 1/4 c Finely chopped onion (dry)
- 2 tbsp Chopped parsley
- 1 egg, slightly beaten
- 1 tsp Salt
- 1/8 to 1/2 tsp Pepper
- 2 lb Ground beef
- 2 slices Process cheese (Mozzarella)

### Instructions

1. **Sauce:** Blend soup, water, oregano, and garlic in a bowl.
2. **Mix:** Combine 1/4 cup of the soup mixture with bread cubes, onion, parsley, egg, salt, and pepper. Mix thoroughly with the ground beef.
3. **Bake:** Place in a loaf pan. Bake for 1 hour at 350°.
4. **Finish:** Remove from oven and spoon off excess fat. Pour the remaining soup mixture over the loaf. Top with cheese slices and return to the oven until cheese melts.
