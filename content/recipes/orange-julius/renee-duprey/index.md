# Orange Julius

#### [Renee DuPrey](/authors/renee-duprey/)

![Orange Julius](/images/orange-julius.webp)

### Description

A light, frothy citrus drink that's sweet, creamy, and refreshingly nostalgic. Made with orange juice concentrate, milk, a touch of vanilla, and ice, this simple blender drink transforms pantry staples into a silky, soda-shop style treat. Serve cold.

### Ingredients

- 1 Cup milk
- 3/4 Cup frozen orange juice concentrate
- 1 Cup water
- 1 teaspoon vanilla
- 1/4 cup sugar
- 10 ice cubes

### Instructions

Combine all ingredients in a blender and blend until smooth and frothy.
