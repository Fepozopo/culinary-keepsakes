# Major Coffee Rub

#### [Peet's Coffee](/authors/peets-coffee/)

![Major Coffee Rub](/images/major-coffee-rub.webp)

### Description

This deeply flavored coffee rub brings the earthy, smoky character of Peet's Major Dickason's coffee to grilled meats and plant-based dishes. Its natural tannins make coffee especially well suited for adding flavor to steaks, spareribs, and tempeh kebabs.

### Ingredients

_All herbs and spices are dried._

- 1 teaspoon black peppercorns
- 1 teaspoon whole coriander
- 3 tablespoons Peet's Major Dickason's coffee, ground extra-fine for espresso
- 2 tablespoons brown sugar
- 1 tablespoon plus 2 teaspoons ancho chile powder
- 2 teaspoons paprika
- 1 teaspoon smoked paprika
- 1 teaspoon marjoram
- 3/4 teaspoon ground ginger
- 1/4 to 1/2 teaspoon chili powder, or more to taste
- 1/4 teaspoon garlic powder
- 1/4 teaspoon onion powder
- 1 1/4 teaspoons kosher salt

### Instructions

1. In a spice grinder, combine the black peppercorns and coriander. Blitz until well ground, then transfer to a medium bowl.
2. Add the remaining ingredients to the bowl and mix very well.
3. Transfer the rub to an airtight container.
