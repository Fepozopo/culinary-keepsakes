# Penicillin

#### [Sassenach Spirits](/authors/sassenach-spirits/)

![Penicillin](/images/penicillin.webp)

### Description

Celebrate World Whisky Day with the Penicillin, a bright and smoky Sassenach cocktail. Zesty lemon, candied ginger, honey-ginger syrup, and a touch of Islay single malt Scotch complement The Sassenach Blended Scotch Whisky.

### Ingredients

- 2 ounces The Sassenach Blended Scotch Whisky
- 3/4 ounce freshly squeezed lemon juice
- 3/4 ounce honey-ginger syrup
- 1/4 ounce Islay single malt Scotch whisky
- Candied ginger, for garnish
- Ice

### Instructions

1. Add the blended Scotch whisky, lemon juice, honey-ginger syrup, and Islay single malt Scotch to a cocktail shaker with ice.
2. Shake, then strain over a large ice cube.
3. Garnish with candied ginger.
