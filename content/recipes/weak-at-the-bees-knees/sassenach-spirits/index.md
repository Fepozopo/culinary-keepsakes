# Weak at the Bee’s Knees

#### [Sassenach Spirits](/authors/sassenach-spirits/)

![Weak at the Bee’s Knees](/images/weak-at-the-bees-knees.webp)

### Description

Honey, lemon, and a little sting! Mix one in, settle in, and get ready for what’s coming.

### Ingredients

- 2 ounces Sassenach Whisky
- 1 ounce lemon
- 3/4 ounce honey syrup
- Chili oil drops, for topping
