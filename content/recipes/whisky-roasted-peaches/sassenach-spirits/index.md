# Whisky Roasted Peaches

#### [Sassenach Spirits](/authors/sassenach-spirits/)

![Whisky Roasted Peaches](/images/whisky-roasted-peaches.webp)

### Ingredients

- 3 yellow peaches
- 1 1/2 tablespoons Sassenach Whisky
- 1 1/2 tablespoons melted butter
- 1 1/2 tablespoons maple syrup
- 1/4 teaspoon cinnamon
- 1 tablespoon roughly chopped pecans
- Vanilla ice cream

### Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 400°F and line a small baking dish with parchment paper. Cut the peaches in half, remove the pits, and arrange the peaches cut-side up in the dish.

2. Whisk the whisky, melted butter, maple syrup, and cinnamon together until smooth. Brush the mixture over the cut sides of the peaches.

![Pouring Sassenach Whisky for the peach glaze](/images/whisky-roasted-peaches-01.webp)
![Brushing the whisky mixture over the cut sides of the peaches](/images/whisky-roasted-peaches-02.webp)

3. Flip the peaches cut-side down and brush the mixture over their backs. Drizzle any remaining mixture over the peaches, then bake for 10 minutes.

![Brushing the whisky mixture over the backs of the peaches](/images/whisky-roasted-peaches-03.webp)

4. Flip the peaches cut-side up and bake for another 15–20 minutes, until soft and caramelized.

![Roasted peaches in the pan with the whisky sauce](/images/whisky-roasted-peaches-04.webp)

5. Serve each peach with a scoop of vanilla ice cream, a sprinkle of pecans, and a drizzle of the warm, buttery syrup from the pan.

![Whisky roasted peaches served with vanilla ice cream and pecans](/images/whisky-roasted-peaches-05.webp)
