# Bear Necessities

#### [Sassenach Spirits](/authors/sassenach-spirits/)

![Bear Necessities](/images/bear-necessities.webp)

### Description

Crisp, bold, and not to be underestimated. Mix one in, settle in, and get ready for what’s coming.

### Ingredients

- 2 ounces Sassenach Gin
- 1 ounce lime juice
- 1/2 ounce simple syrup
- 6–8 mint leaves
- Soda, for topping
